Services

Pumps, Motors & Gear Reducers

Q² Machines supplies, repairs and services pumps, AC motors and shaft mounted gear reducers for industrial and commercial applications, from our facility in Trinidad.

What we supply

  • Centrifugal Pumps
  • Submersible Pumps
  • Self-Priming Pumps
  • Slurry & Sludge Pumps
  • Dredge Pumps
  • Progressive Cavity Pumps
  • Pump Spare Parts & Seals
  • AC Motor Supply for Industrial & Commercial Applications
  • Shaft Mounted Gear Reducers
  • Spares & Hardware Supply — Bearings, Rebuild Kits, Seals, Guards, Motor Stands

Built in-house

Repair and service work is carried out from our facility in Trinidad, where our machine shop and fabrication and welding workshop are also based. We serve clients across the Caribbean, Venezuela, Colombia, Guyana and South America.

Frequently asked questions

What types of pumps do you supply?

We supply centrifugal, submersible, self-priming, slurry and sludge, dredge and progressive cavity pumps, along with pump spare parts and seals.

Can you rewind or repair motors and gear reducers?

Yes. We offer motor rewind and rebuild, motor testing and inspection, gearbox strip-down and repair, bearing and seal replacement, vibration analysis and balancing, and onsite repairs.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes. We offer preventive maintenance contracts for motors and gear reducers, and preventive maintenance for pumps.
