Gear Reducers

Shaft-mounted gear reducers are the workhorses of industrial power transmission. They mount directly onto the driven machine’s shaft through a hollow bore, with a torque arm to maintain the gear reducer in a stable position during operation. With no foot mounting or base plate needed, they install quickly and easily. They are widely used on conveyors, bucket elevators, mixers, fine and coarse material washers and similar equipment. Q² Machines supplies, services and supports OCR shaft-mounted gear reducers from Modena, Italy.
